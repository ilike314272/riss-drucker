# Case study: H₂O₂ torsion–rotation from ab initio, built dynamically

Reproduce:
1. `python scripts/case_h2o2_abinitio.py --out DIR --relaxed` writes the relaxed scan point sets
   and the earlier 1-D baseline.
2. `python scripts/case_h2o2_rph.py --scan DIR` builds the dynamic model and runs the update.

Derived numbers are in `h2o2_rph_results.json` and, for the 1-D baseline,
`h2o2_abinitio_results.json`. Reference levels are from Hunt et al., *J. Chem. Phys.* 42, 1931
(1965).

## What was wrong with the earlier treatment

The first version treated H₂O₂ as a fixed model type: a 1-D torsion with a constant torsional
constant F = 36.4 cm⁻¹, taken from the OH arm length at the minimum. Two things were wrong:
1. **The kinetic energy.** The torsional coefficient of p² is not a constant. From the exact
   generalized inertia tensor of the relaxed geometries it is 41.7 cm⁻¹ at cis and 38.5 cm⁻¹ at
   trans. In the rigid limit, removing the centre-of-mass motion alone raises it 6% above the
   textbook 2/(m_H r⊥²); `tests/python/test_dynamic_builder.py` checks that to 10⁻⁴. Away from trans the torsion also couples to rotation about the C2 axis.
2. **The missing physics.** There was no torsion–rotation coupling (J_x p, J_yJ_z terms and
   γ-dependent rotational constants), no pseudopotential, no adiabatic correction from the other
   modes, and no K dependence. In the bisector frame, K-odd states have antiperiodic torsional
   functions.

The scan's angle axis was right: the actual HOOH dihedral of every relaxed geometry equals the
scan label to 10⁻⁴°. That is checked in the script, which always uses the measured dihedral.

## The dynamic model

Nothing below is a hand-picked model type; the pieces are assembled from the geometry, the
energies and the group:
- **Spaces:** rotation |J k⟩ ⊗ torsion e^{i(m/2)γ}, with γ the full HOOH dihedral on [0, 4π).
- **Group:** G8(H2O2), generated by the ends-swap C2x (γ→γ), E* (C2y, γ→−γ) and the gauge
  element (γ→γ+2π, C2z) of the half-angle frame. Physical blocks A1, A2, B1, B2; spin weights
  1, 1, 3, 3.
- **Kinetic terms** (`rd.abinitio.rph`):
  - the generalized inertia tensor of the body frame is built from the 19 relaxed MP2/cc-pVDZ
    geometries, extended to 4π by the group operations and Fourier-interpolated (max fit error
    5.7×10⁻⁵ Å);
  - G = I⁻¹ gives every p², J_aJ_b and J_a{·,p} coefficient;
  - the Podolsky pseudopotential is −1.4 … +1.0 cm⁻¹;
  - forbidden elements vanish at cis/trans to 10⁻¹⁵.
- **Potential:** an MP2/cc-pVDZ surface plus a GP Δ to CCSD(T)/cc-pVTZ (7 points), on the
  actual dihedrals. Optionally it adds the adiabatic ΔZPE from MP2/cc-pVDZ Hessians projected on
  translations, rotations and the path tangent.
- **Terms:** every coefficient function is expanded only in the Fourier functions for which its
  operator is invariant, decided by the compiler's own symmetry test. Half-integer orders appear
  exactly with K-odd operators. Result: 49 terms, all compile certificates passed.

## Results: J = 0 torsional levels (cm⁻¹ above the ground level)

| Model | τ-splitting | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Earlier 1-D, fixed F, nominal angles | 10.88 | 244.85 | 353.58 | 544.17 | 740.42 |
| MP2/cc-pVDZ only (dynamic model) | 26.2 | 226.3 | 379.7 | 583.6 | 805.0 |
| **Dynamic model, CCSD(T) Δ, no ZPE** | **12.62** | **250.54** | **368.75** | **567.81** | **774.66** |
| … without the pseudopotential | 10.06 | 259.69 | 368.11 | 566.91 | 770.46 |
| … with harmonic adiabatic ZPE | 10.02 | 259.62 | 367.82 | 566.64 | 770.26 |
| **Bayesian update (posterior)** | **11.91** | **254.86** | **370.10** | **569.73** | **775.94** |
| Experiment (Hunt 1965) | 11.4 | 254.6 | 370.9 | 569.5 | 775.9 |

The rows labelled "… without" and "… with" both include the harmonic ZPE; they differ from the
ZPE row only in the pseudopotential. The propagated GP standard deviation of the levels is
≤ 0.04 cm⁻¹.

- **Torsion levels.** The excited levels move from 4–5% low to 0.15–1.6% (dynamic model, no ZPE), with
  no parameter fitted to spectra. The fix is the kinetic physics; the ab initio surface is
  unchanged.
- **Harmonic ZPE overshoots.** The MP2/cc-pVDZ harmonic adiabatic ZPE raises the trans barrier
  from 371 to 404 cm⁻¹ and shrinks the splitting to 10.0. A harmonic, small-basis ZPE along a
  floppy path is not reliable at the 10 cm⁻¹ level. The "no ZPE" model is closer, but that may be
  partly cancellation (the CCSD(T)/cc-pVTZ barrier is not at the basis-set limit).
- **Rotation.** The J = 1 K = 0 level is at 1.73 cm⁻¹ (B + C; experiment ≈ 1.71). The K = 1
  levels come out near A + (B + C)/2, and the torsional splitting depends on K through the
  antiperiodic K-odd functions. These are not compared with data here.

## Bayesian update (M5)

The update starts from the model with harmonic ZPE. It corrects V1, V2 and V3 (the cos γ, cos 2γ,
cos 3γ coefficients) against Hunt's five transitions (σ = 0.5 cm⁻¹).
- **Prior:** sd = GP sd (≈0.03–0.05 cm⁻¹) combined in quadrature with an ab initio model error
  τ_ab.
- **Choosing τ_ab:** type-II maximum likelihood. The marginal likelihood of the linearized model
  is exact Gaussian: y − y₀ ~ N(0, σ²I + A S Aᵀ). It peaks at τ_ab ≈ 10–30 cm⁻¹ (chosen: 10).
  So the data say the ab initio potential's low-order cosine coefficients are uncertain at
  about 10–30 cm⁻¹, far above the GP's interpolation uncertainty. This is the model error that
  the GP variance alone cannot show.
- **Posterior shifts:** +13.8, −15.1, −7.4 cm⁻¹, i.e. 1.38, −1.51 and −0.74 prior sd. That is
  within the prior: no conflict between the ab initio prior and the data.
- **Leave-one-out:** each level predicted from the other four lands within 2.6 cm⁻¹: 11.98, 257.17,
  369.90, 569.85, 776.00 against 11.4, 254.6, 370.9, 569.5, 775.9.

## Torsion–rotation structure vs the JPL catalogue

The catalogue is JPL tag 034004 (Petkie et al., J. Mol. Spectrosc. 171, 145 (1995); Camy-Peyret et
al. (1992)). It yields 5,667 levels from 38,357 lines, with combination differences consistent to
1×10⁻⁴ cm⁻¹. Run with `scripts/case_h2o2_rph.py --jpl c034004.cat`.

The comparison covers 101 n = 0 levels with J ≤ 8 and Ka ≤ 3, both torsional components
(catalogue v = 0 and 3). Assignment is by quantum number: Ka from ⟨J_z²⟩, the torsional component
from energy order within each (J, Ka), and Kc by prolate ordering.

- **Pure ab initio prediction** (dynamic model with ZPE, no spectroscopic input): 1.30 cm⁻¹ rms.
  By Ka: 0.90, 0.94, 1.25, 1.89. The error is dominated by the torsional splitting, 10.02 vs
  11.44 cm⁻¹.
- **Joint Bayesian update:** fit to J ≤ 5 (59 levels, σ = 0.02 cm⁻¹) of the potential V1–V3,
  the three rotational G constants, and the first two cos-coefficients of G_zz(γ), i.e. the
  γ-dependence of A.
  - Fit rms 0.005 cm⁻¹. The 42 held-out levels with J = 6–8 are predicted to 0.017 cm⁻¹ rms
    (max 0.041).
  - All shifts are within 1.3 prior sd.

**The K dependence of the torsional splitting** (cm⁻¹):

| | J=1, Ka=0 | J=1, Ka=1 | J=2, Ka=2 | J=3, Ka=3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Observed | 11.4384 | 11.4282 | 11.3999 | 11.3528 |
| Ab initio | 10.0236 | 10.0229 | 10.0236 | 10.0242 |
| After update | 11.4397 | 11.4292 | 11.4007 | 11.3528 |

The ab initio model has the right structure: the K-odd antiperiodic functions and the
γ-dependent rotational constants. But it predicts almost no K dependence. With only the constant
parts of G free, the update could not reproduce the trend. Freeing the γ-dependence of G_zz
reproduces it, so the MP2/cc-pVDZ geometry underestimates how strongly A changes along the torsion
path. That is a concrete target for Δ-learning the kinetic terms.

## Limits

- Torsion is the only active coordinate; the other modes enter adiabatically (ΔZPE, harmonic).
- Kinetic terms are MP2/cc-pVDZ geometry only, with no Δ-learning of G.
- The kinetic terms are not Δ-learned: G_zz(γ) is corrected by the data, not by a higher ab initio
  level.
- The Hunt levels themselves come from a 1965 analysis.

## Earlier 1-D baseline (kept for comparison)

`case_h2o2_abinitio.py --relaxed` fits a GP on the nominal scan coordinate and uses a fixed F
from the minimum geometry. Its levels are the first row of the table above. The leave-one-out
z-scores of its seven high-level points (−1.26 … 1.69) showed the GP is honest about
interpolation. What it missed is listed under "What was wrong with the earlier treatment".
